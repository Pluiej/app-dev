## **Game of Thrones**
## About
*Game of Thrones* is a fantasy television series on HBO that aired from 2011 to 2019, based on George R. R. Martin's book series *A Song of Ice and Fire*.
### Genre
Fantasy drama, with elements of action and political intrigue.

### Premise
The series is about the struggle for power among several noble houses in *Westeros*, a continent with seven(7) kingdoms, all vying to control the Iron Throne.
